# Issue #782 — Tranche Strategy Review (`da8588a`)

## Operational caveats

The junior-exit finding requires a holder whose cooldown is already mature and whose withdrawal window is open when a parent loss becomes observable. The [proposed deployment](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-tranche-strategy/blob/da8588afc1f1e79176c658e3e2e44c0beb06d4e5/script/Deploy.s.sol#L65-L68) uses 14-day and 30-day junior cooldowns, so a holder cannot begin a cooldown after seeing a loss and immediately exit. Keeper latency and actual cooldown state affect likelihood; the example demonstrates impact, not an easy on-demand exit.

The post-cover stale-price finding requires separate successful tranche reports, or a batch that allows a later report to fail without reverting earlier reports. A single atomic, all-or-nothing senior-to-junior batch that completes every report closes that inter-report window. The reviewed contracts expose individual reports and do not enforce such a batch. The default junior loss health check can reject the batch until management intervenes.

The senior lossy-redemption finding may be an intentional design tradeoff if users knowingly accept loss before cover is collected; explicit zero-loss redemption reverts. These qualifications do not establish deployed exposure. Contract-level tests and runtime configuration remain unverified.

## [High] Junior withdrawals can evade senior coverage obligations

### Details

[`availableWithdrawLimit()` and `_freeFunds()`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-tranche-strategy/blob/da8588afc1f1e79176c658e3e2e44c0beb06d4e5/src/Strategy.sol#L135-L149) permit exits without reserving outstanding senior cover. With senior/junior claims of 120/120, a recognized parent loss of 60 leaves each position worth 90. Before senior reporting, an eligible junior holder can withdraw its entire 90 backing, leaving only 90 for seniors. Reconciliation first would allocate 120/60. A mature cooldown suffices; compromised privileges and withdrawal slippage are unnecessary.

### Recommendation

Enforce priority-adjusted entitlement before junior withdrawals. Reconcile observable parent losses and coverage obligations before permitting exits, including during shutdown.

## [High] Cover payments leave junior shares redeemable at an overstated price

### Details

[`cover()`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-tranche-strategy/blob/da8588afc1f1e79176c658e3e2e44c0beb06d4e5/src/Strategy.sol#L196-L207) transfers backing without updating the payer’s stored assets or restricting withdrawals. After senior cover leaves junior backing of 60 against 120 assets/shares, two holders with 60 shares each are still quoted 60 each. The first can withdraw all backing, leaving the second nothing instead of 30. The default health check rejects the junior’s corrective loss report until management intervenes. Withdrawals with `maxLoss=0` remain exploitable because the stale quoted amount is paid fully.

### Recommendation

Synchronize cover transfers with each payer’s loss accounting atomically, or suspend affected deposits and withdrawals until proportional pricing is restored.

## [Medium] Senior redemption can permanently extinguish unpaid cover

### Details

When an underlying strategy recognizes loss before the parent processes its report, parent conversions remain nominal while [redemption realizes the loss](https://github.com/yearn/yearn-vaults-v3/blob/104a2b233bc6d43ba40720d68355b04d2dc31795/contracts/VaultV3.vy#L800-L898). A default senior redemption can therefore burn a 120 claim while returning 90. [Tokenized Strategy removes proceeds plus loss from accounting](https://github.com/yearn/tokenized-strategy/blob/82806289f967590c4efbf6bc3d237e4e7f0a0966/src/TokenizedStrategy.sol#L1020-L1044), leaving no shortfall for a later report to cover despite funded juniors. Explicit zero-loss redemption reverts and preserves the claim.

### Recommendation

Reconcile upstream losses and collect cover before finalizing senior exits, or retain an enforceable claim for unpaid coverage. Reject lossy exits that would otherwise erase that entitlement.

## [Low] Idle assets invalidate advertised deposit capacity

### Details

[`availableDepositLimit()`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-tranche-strategy/blob/da8588afc1f1e79176c658e3e2e44c0beb06d4e5/src/Strategy.sol#L128-L132) returns parent headroom, but Tokenized Strategy deploys the complete loose balance. With parent headroom of 100 and an existing donation of 100 underlying, the tranche advertises 100 while even a deposit of 1 attempts to route 101 and reverts. This temporarily blocks deposits and misleads capacity-based allocation.

### Recommendation

Account for existing idle assets when calculating parent deployment capacity, and verify that deposits at the advertised maximum succeed.
