# Flex Lender post-anchor security review

## [Medium] Recoverable cooldown value can be reported as loss or misallocated between holders

### Details

At `70b9f835`, an in-kind auction take leaves collateral untracked while `pendingRedemptions` remains zero; the report path values only base asset and Lender shares. Management can also clear a live cooldown counter without settling its external claim, and fxSAVE clears the counter before its returned fxUSD is converted. If a loss-permitting/bypassed report, or a vault withdrawal after a short claim, occurs before settlement, recoverable value is booked as loss or earlier withdrawers avoid a disproportionate share; later recovery becomes new profit and can incur performance fees. Default zero-loss health checks and privileged sequencing reduce exposure but do not enforce correct settlement.

### Recommendation

Use an explicit exit-state machine that blocks reports and withdrawals relying on the position until all collateral/claims are conservatively valued, converted, and reported atomically. Remove unrestricted counter clearing or restrict it to a proven irreversible write-off, and add Vault V3 regressions for in-kind, short-claim, fxUSD, and interleaved-withdrawal paths.

## [Medium] Nested Lender losses can make advertised exits revert

### Details

`availableWithdrawLimit()` uses the whole Lender’s idle base-asset balance, while `_freeFunds()` redeems shares calculated at the Lender’s current PPS without capping them to this strategy’s own share balance. After an inner-Lender loss is reported but before the outer strategy reports, another Lender holder’s idle liquidity can make the outer strategy advertise an amount that requires more Lender shares than it owns; the ordinary outer/Vault V3 exit then reverts instead of synchronizing the loss.

### Recommendation

Cap the advertised immediate liquidity by both global Lender idle balance and this strategy’s current redeemable Lender position, and cap `_freeFunds()` accordingly. Add a multi-holder Lender regression that triggers a lower inner PPS, verifies the advertised limit, and exercises an ordinary Vault V3 withdrawal.

## [Medium] Auction and cooldown settlement have no bounded recovery path

### Details

The final `kick_timestamp == 0` check correctly prevents an expired, untaken auction from being reported as a loss, but it also blocks reporting and further forced frees until the entire lot is taken; cooldown variants similarly block while pending. Public re-kicks exist, but the prepared evidence contains no market-depth, queue-liveness, or deployment-operations proof that a buyer/claim will arrive within a bounded time.

### Recommendation

Before deployment, test repeated unfilled re-kicks and unavailable cooldown claims at intended position sizes, document monitoring and intervention owners, and provide a bounded conservative recovery mechanism that cannot misreport recoverable value.
