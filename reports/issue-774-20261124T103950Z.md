# Aave V4 Lender Borrower Strategy Review — `c5eda78`

## [Medium] Zero collateral factor makes reports revert

### Details

The strategy divides by its target LTV in [`availableDepositLimit`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-aave-v4-lender-borrower-strategy/blob/c5eda7812656682470b75b375f35054a3b524c3c/src/BaseLenderBorrower.sol#L380-L395), which every report calls. The prepared [Aave Spoke implementation](https://github.com/aave/aave-v4/blob/c1856d565a7b63b7c7e75a2797064eb981af15f4/src/spoke/Spoke.sol#L191-L203) permits a new dynamic configuration with a zero collateral factor. Such an offboarding change makes reports and deposit-limit queries revert, even when no new Aave action is needed.

### Recommendation

Return zero deposit capacity when target LTV is zero while preserving reporting and recovery. Test an open position after Aave adds a zero-factor configuration.

## [Medium] Keeper calls can re-leverage after shutdown

### Details

The pinned Yearn framework leaves `report()` and `tend()` callable after shutdown. Both reach [`_leveragePosition`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-aave-v4-lender-borrower-strategy/blob/c5eda7812656682470b75b375f35054a3b524c3c/src/BaseLenderBorrower.sol#L449-L507) without a shutdown check. After emergency recovery leaves loose collateral or an under-target position, a keeper can resupply collateral, borrow, and relend during wind-down.

### Recommendation

Block new supply, borrowing, and lending after shutdown while retaining valuation and repayment. Test both keeper calls after `emergencyWithdraw()`.

## [Medium] A paused debt reserve makes advertised exits revert

### Details

[`availableWithdrawLimit`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-aave-v4-lender-borrower-strategy/blob/c5eda7812656682470b75b375f35054a3b524c3c/src/Strategy.sol#L257-L269) checks collateral exit state but not whether Aave permits debt repayment. It can advertise an exit requiring repayment while the borrow-token reserve is paused; the actual `Spoke.repay` call [reverts on that pause](https://github.com/aave/aave-v4/blob/c1856d565a7b63b7c7e75a2797064eb981af15f4/src/spoke/Spoke.sol#L878-L880). Direct withdrawals and a parent Vault queue can fail despite the quoted capacity.

### Recommendation

Limit exits to collateral retrievable without repayment while repayment is disabled. Test small unencumbered and larger debt-requiring exits under a debt-reserve pause.

## [Medium] A large lender loss can block loss reporting

### Details

When lender assets fall materially below Aave debt, [`_settleDebt`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-aave-v4-lender-borrower-strategy/blob/c5eda7812656682470b75b375f35054a3b524c3c/src/BaseLenderBorrower.sol#L239-L255) withdraws the collateral estimated for the full debt-token purchase before repaying debt. The prepared Aave Spoke [checks health factor after withdrawal](https://github.com/aave/aave-v4/blob/c1856d565a7b63b7c7e75a2797064eb981af15f4/src/spoke/Spoke.sol#L244-L265). If that withdrawal breaches the collateral threshold, `report()` reverts before recognizing the loss, even with Yearn’s health check disabled.

### Recommendation

Settle large shortfalls in health-safe stages or report conservatively without requiring full settlement. Test a lender impairment beyond Aave’s immediately withdrawable collateral.

## [Medium] Default forced leverage can sustain negative carry

### Details

[`forceLeverage` defaults to true](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-aave-v4-lender-borrower-strategy/blob/c5eda7812656682470b75b375f35054a3b524c3c/src/Strategy.sol#L23-L25), making the strategy report zero borrow APR and positive lender APR to its leverage checks. The proposed deployment script also sets it true. If actual Aave debt cost exceeds lender and collateral yield, the strategy can add or retain losing debt. The prepared evidence does not establish a deployed flag or live rate spread.

### Recommendation

Disable forced leverage by default unless a governed controller independently enforces positive carry. Test high borrow cost against low lender yield with the flag on and off.

## [Low] Reports can attempt disabled Aave actions

### Details

The [report hook](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-aave-v4-lender-borrower-strategy/blob/c5eda7812656682470b75b375f35054a3b524c3c/src/BaseLenderBorrower.sol#L224-L236) always settles debt and checks leverage. With an under-target position, it can attempt borrowing despite a paused, frozen, or non-borrowable debt reserve; with a lender shortfall, it can attempt repayment during a debt-reserve pause. The reached [Aave validations](https://github.com/aave/aave-v4/blob/c1856d565a7b63b7c7e75a2797064eb981af15f4/src/spoke/Spoke.sol#L867-L880) reject those calls, delaying accounting reports.

### Recommendation

Make reporting skip forbidden actions and record a conservative position value. Test an under-target position with borrowing disabled and a shortfall with repayment paused.

## [Low] Collateral APR quote omits premium-share accrual

### Details

The optional [APR oracle](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-aave-v4-lender-borrower-strategy/blob/c5eda7812656682470b75b375f35054a3b524c3c/src/periphery/StrategyAprOracle.sol#L136-L162) uses Aave’s net owed amount to weight collateral supplier yield. In the prepared Aave implementation, gross premium shares accrue with the drawn index while their initial offset makes net premium debt zero. With material premium shares, the oracle can underquote yield and misrank allocations; deployment use and magnitude are unverified.

### Recommendation

Model gross premium-share accrual and compare the quote with measured supplier-asset growth and allocator decisions at the intended deployment.

## [Informational] Lender liquidity can affect report liveness and valuation

### Details

The strategy adds one unit to its lender withdrawal quote, so a zero `maxRedeem` quote can trigger redemption during a profitable report. With partial liquidity, [settlement](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-aave-v4-lender-borrower-strategy/blob/c5eda7812656682470b75b375f35054a3b524c3c/src/BaseLenderBorrower.sol#L239-L248) can leave positive lender equity out of the reported value, potentially shifting prior gains to later depositors. Generic Vault V3 permits the relevant liquidity states, but the intended yvUSD runtime and queue were not prepared; neither outcome is confirmed for that deployment.

### Recommendation

Pin the intended yvUSD implementation. Test reports at zero and partial `maxRedeem`, then add a second holder through the actual parent Vault and measure claims after liquidity returns.

## [Informational] Deployed strategy and external custody remain unverified

### Details

No prepared bytecode comparison, concrete parent Vault, or exact yvUSD and MetaExchange runtime and route proves this source is deployed or that its [maximum swap allowance](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-aave-v4-lender-borrower-strategy/blob/c5eda7812656682470b75b375f35054a3b524c3c/src/BaseLenderBorrower.sol#L918-L929) is safe. ### Recommendation
