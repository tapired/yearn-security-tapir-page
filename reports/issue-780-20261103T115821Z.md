# yTranche Security Review — `58182cf`

## [High] Junior exits can transfer unsettled losses to senior holders

### Details

[`withdrawFromTranche()`](https://github.com/yearn/ytranche/blob/58182cf41f38aa86365942a23af4876fbd76fca2/src/TrancheController.sol#L432-L455) pays against nominal tranche claims without enforcing the loss waterfall. After parent loss recognition, senior/junior claims of 1,000/1,000 backed by 1,500 let an eligible junior withdraw 1,000 before settlement, leaving only 500 for the senior. Settling first would preserve the senior’s 1,000 and reduce the junior to 500. Mature cooldowns and sufficient liquidity make this reachable without privileged compromise.

### Recommendation

Reconcile observable upstream losses and enforce priority-adjusted entitlement before pricing and paying redemptions.

## [High] Health-check floors let early holders consume settled backing

### Details

The pinned [`BaseHealthCheck`](https://github.com/yearn/tokenized-strategy-periphery/blob/eadf6aa2b3dc20446948d4c4918e9016c0d8ecde/src/Bases/HealthCheck/BaseHealthCheck.sol#L150-L182) substitutes a minimum asset value for actual NAV. The default zero loss ratio preserves the old price after controller settlement. With 1,000 shares and backing reduced to 500, the first holder can redeem 500 shares for all remaining backing instead of 250. Old shares can similarly consume fresh recapitalization after a wipe. The controller’s aggregate withdrawal cap does not protect individual holders.

### Recommendation

Price shares from actual loss-adjusted NAV. Halt affected flows when losses require review, and synchronize controller settlement with tranche accounting atomically.

## [Medium] Timestamp caching preserves prices across standalone settlement

### Details

Pinned [`TokenizedStrategy`](https://github.com/yearn/tokenized-strategy/blob/8c8929f1878e8c5ad78aa0a6dabc877a890f68d9/src/TokenizedStrategy.sol#L1195-L1200) skips accounting synchronization after the first operation at a timestamp. Controller settlement changes entitlement without invalidating that cache. Even with permissive loss limits, a holder can perform a small operation before settlement and redeem afterward at the old price in the same block, shifting losses to remaining holders. A successful atomic settlement/report batch closes this window, but standalone settlement remains available.

### Recommendation

Invalidate cached accounting whenever settlement changes entitlement, or enforce atomic settlement and tranche synchronization before user operations resume.

## [Medium] An empty excess recipient blocks the keeper batch

### Details

[`settleAndReport()`](https://github.com/yearn/ytranche/blob/58182cf41f38aa86365942a23af4876fbd76fca2/src/periphery/Keeper.sol#L19-L26) reports every registered tranche after settlement. An empty tranche can receive pending excess, but realizing it fails the inherited health check: its profit ceiling remains zero because prior assets are zero, regardless of the configured ratio. The failure rolls back settlement and every preceding report. Normal reconciliation then requires management intervention or new deposits.

### Recommendation

Define ownership of zero-supply earnings and handle empty recipients without blocking reconciliation of funded tranches.

## [Medium] Shutdown bypasses funded reserve protection

### Details

[`_drawReserveToMain()`](https://github.com/yearn/ytranche/blob/58182cf41f38aa86365942a23af4876fbd76fca2/src/TrancheController.sol#L598-L612) returns zero after parent shutdown, while settlement continues reducing user claims. With claims of 70, parent NAV of 65, and a liquid reserve of 10, shutdown followed by settlement leaves users with 65 and the reserve untouched. The prepared emergency test explicitly asserts this behavior. Withdrawals remain open, and exiting users retain no automatic claim to subsequent reserve compensation.

### Recommendation

Preserve reserve-backed entitlements during shutdown through a settlement or compensation path that does not require depositing into the closed parent.

## [Low] Idle assets bypass tranche limits and invalidate deposit quotes

### Details

Deposits deploy the entire loose underlying balance through [`_deployFunds()`](https://github.com/yearn/ytranche/blob/58182cf41f38aa86365942a23af4876fbd76fca2/src/TrancheStrategy.sol#L105-L128), while tranche admission and throughput metering use only the declared deposit. An existing sole holder can donate assets and make a small deposit to exceed local ceilings and rate limits. Conversely, parent headroom of 10 with one idle asset still advertises a deposit of 10, then attempts to route 11 and reverts. Parent ingress limits remain enforced.

### Recommendation

Check and meter the full routed balance, including idle assets, and adjust advertised deposit capacity accordingly.

## [Low] The zero-mint guard strands redeemed reserve assets

### Details

The controller [redeems reserve shares before checking parent mintability](https://github.com/yearn/ytranche/blob/58182cf41f38aa86365942a23af4876fbd76fca2/src/TrancheController.sol#L602-L608). If the proceeds mint zero parent shares, it returns zero while retaining underlying excluded from backing calculations. Settlement then charges the apparent shortfall to users. At ordinary share prices this is rounding-sized, and governance can recover the loose assets manually.

### Recommendation

Check mintability before reserve redemption, or account for and reuse retained underlying without charging users for it.

## [Low] Configuring the parent as reserve creates fictitious coverage

### Details

[`setReserveVault()`](https://github.com/yearn/ytranche/blob/58182cf41f38aa86365942a23af4876fbd76fca2/src/TrancheController.sol#L221-L228) permits the main vault itself. Both backing getters then count the same shares. During loss settlement, redeeming and redepositing those shares produces a positive reported reserve draw without adding backing, so claims remain inflated. This requires governance misconfiguration; no intended deployment using it was established.

### Recommendation

Reject self-referential reserve configurations and validate that reserve backing is economically separate from the parent position.
