# Monolith SWAN Compounder Review — `4b103e3`

## [Low] Expired unsold rewards require fresh rewards before relisting

### Details

Both `kickAuction()` and reporting check only strategy-held rewards before restarting an auction. Expired inventory remains in the auction and cannot trigger either path. With the configured 100-swanUSD threshold, even substantial unsold inventory waits until fresh rewards exceed 100. Direct keeper kicks are prohibited by auction governance. The [resulting strategy](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-monolith-compounder-strategy/blob/4b103e32253df396ce8e751a19a3dd590120a290/src/Strategy.sol#L167-L198) retains the reported defect. Additional rewards or a funded workaround restore progress.

### Recommendation

Allow inactive auction inventory to trigger relisting independently of fresh rewards. Transfer only positive fresh balances, and test zero and below-threshold claims.

## [Low] Final shutdown redemption can orphan late auction proceeds

### Details

Pending reward auctions are excluded from strategy assets. After shutdown and principal recovery, the final shareholder can redeem every share before an auction pays SWAN. Those proceeds then reach a permanently closed strategy with no shareholders or exposed underlying-rescue function. Reporting cannot recreate former holders’ entitlement; bypassing the zero-baseline health check only updates accounting. This affects residual yield and is avoidable through settlement ordering. See the [reporting path](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-monolith-compounder-strategy/blob/4b103e32253df396ce8e751a19a3dd590120a290/src/Strategy.sol#L194-L217) and [redemption accounting](https://github.com/yearn/tokenized-strategy/blob/48c53aba0395885b3f579d0a4b426de276a7b69e/src/TokenizedStrategy.sol#L1131-L1191).

### Recommendation

Settle and report outstanding rewards before final redemption, or preserve beneficiary entitlements through a narrowly scoped residual-proceeds recovery mechanism. Test parent debt-zeroing and revocation.

## [Low] Late parent deposits share previously earned rewards

### Details

Public parent deposits receive shares against stored accounting that excludes unrecognized rewards and auction proceeds. Later reporting distributes that earlier yield across the enlarged supply. With 1,000 existing principal, 100 unrecognized rewards and an equal late deposit, the newcomer receives 50 of the earlier rewards after unlocking, before fees and subsequent yield. The configured three-day lock imposes exposure but does not preserve cohort entitlement. The [deployment configuration](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-monolith-compounder-strategy/blob/4b103e32253df396ce8e751a19a3dd590120a290/script/Deploy.s.sol#L70-L82) permits this parent entry path.

### Recommendation

Quantify and explicitly accept this timing dilution, or recognize/reserve accrued value before issuing new parent shares. Validate the two-depositor sequence with actual fees and profit unlocking.

## [Low] Oversized reward lots start below the auction floor

### Details

The script sets a fixed 100,000-SWAN whole-lot starting price and a one-SWAN-per-swanUSD floor. The strategy auctions its entire balance together with unsold inventory. Under the pinned [auction arithmetic](https://github.com/yearn/tokenized-strategy-periphery/blob/1e334fbdd5955a50c085ec3662c6051efb7fc615/src/Auctions/Auction.sol#L295-L319), a 100,001-swanUSD lot starts inactive; a 100,000-token lot becomes inactive after the first decay step. Management can reconfigure pricing, so this is recoverable reward-conversion failure. Production lot sizes and market depth were not established.

### Recommendation

Tranche rewards or scale the total starting price with inventory. Check that opening prices clear the floor, including accumulated expired inventory.

## [Informational] Monolith backing, withdrawal and principal-auction guarantees remain unverified

### Details

The mandatory [Monolith snapshot](https://github.com/MonolithMarket/Monolith/tree/3511ab54c7532efde0fe19423cb3753ea469a805) provides market reserve collection but lacks the exact sSWAN and RevenueRouter implementations. Consequently, fee-free 1:1 backing, withdrawal independence, distribution-failure propagation and accurate availability limits remain unverified. The strategy ignores staking operation booleans and claims before every report, including shutdown reports. It also omits sSWAN from auction protection; actual transferability and principal-sale consequences cannot be decided from interfaces.

### Recommendation

Pin the matching implementations and deployment identities. Test exact token deltas, distribution failures, partial/full withdrawals and emergency recovery. Permanently exclude sSWAN from auction enablement and execution.

## [Informational] APR allocation safety remains unverified

### Details

The [APR oracle](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-monolith-compounder-strategy/blob/4b103e32253df396ce8e751a19a3dd590120a290/src/periphery/MonolithSwanAprOracle.sol#L73-L138) uses instantaneous pool pricing or an authorized manual price without expiry or bounds. The deployer retains manual-price authority after governance transfer. A reduction equal to total staking supply also reverts before reaching the zero-supply return. These behaviors are source-confirmed, but actual registration, consuming allocator behavior and profitable manipulation were not established.

### Recommendation

Trace the registered consumers and test manipulation, stale manual prices and sole-staker full-exit simulations. Apply suitable price/freshness controls and define safe zero-supply quoting.
