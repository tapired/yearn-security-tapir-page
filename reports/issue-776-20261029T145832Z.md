# Issue 776: Alchemix Automated Transmuter

## [Medium] Management can reprice idle alAsset and capture depositor principal

### Details

At prepared commit [`b6305a3`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-automated-transmuter-strategy/blob/b6305a34c8ec99589982936b1b1cb1b5677a1b09/src/Strategy.sol#L180), `setAuctionPrices()` immediately changes idle-alAsset valuation. With material idle inventory and deposit headroom, compromised management can bypass health checks, report an artificial loss, deposit controlled funds, restore valuation and unlock recovery profit. Integer accounting demonstrates approximately 450,000 USDC transferred from legacy holders after a 100,000-USDC controlled deposit into the modeled million-USDC strategy. Frozen position prices limit exposure to idle inventory.

### Recommendation

Separate accounting valuation from mutable auction settings. Prevent deposits during material valuation changes and test the complete loss-report, deposit and restoration sequence with two holders.

## [Medium] Keepers can create fresh positions after shutdown

### Details

[`_tend()` and `_transmutableAmount()`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-automated-transmuter-strategy/blob/b6305a34c8ec99589982936b1b1cb1b5677a1b09/src/Strategy.sol#L503) permit new Transmuter positions after shutdown. Given idle alAsset, capacity and a free slot, a keeper can replace an immediate emergency-sale option with a fresh maturation period. Recovery then waits or requires privileged early claiming with any configured exit fee. The supplied shutdown test explicitly expects this behavior.

### Recommendation

Disable new position creation during shutdown while preserving mature claims, MYT redemption and auction recovery. Test shutdown followed by keeper tending and emergency early claims.

## [Medium] Auction prices can permit purchases of impaired alAsset at a loss

### Details

[`kickAuction()`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-automated-transmuter-strategy/blob/b6305a34c8ec99589982936b1b1cb1b5677a1b09/src/Strategy.sol#L438) uses nominal prices without adjusting for transmutation fees or bad debt. At a 1.15 opening price, 1% fee and 0.85 backing multiplier, eventual recovery is only 0.967725 asset per asset sold. A permissionless taker can fill an outstanding lot under those conditions. Shutdown prevents future kicks but does not cancel active auctions. Current impairment is unverified.

### Recommendation

Enforce a conservative net-recovery bound and protect active lots against changing protocol conditions. Test impairment after kick, shutdown, attempted settlement and explicit auction cancellation.

## [Medium] MYT valuation failures can block withdrawals backed by idle assets

### Details

[`availableWithdrawLimit()`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-automated-transmuter-strategy/blob/b6305a34c8ec99589982936b1b1cb1b5677a1b09/src/Strategy.sol#L231) unconditionally calls `MYT.convertToAssets()`, including with zero MYT and no matured positions. The prepared VaultV2 implementation evaluates registered adapters during valuation. If an adapter valuation reverts, withdrawals can fail despite sufficient idle USDC, potentially interrupting a parent withdrawal queue. Replacing `mytLimits` cannot contain this earlier call. A currently configured failing adapter was not established.

### Recommendation

Short-circuit valuation when no relevant exposure exists and preserve independently known idle liquidity on valuation failure. Test the exact composition with a reverting adapter and an idle-only strategy.

## [Low] Donations can exhaust position slots through keeper automation

### Details

With the script’s 100-alUSD minimum and seven default slots, seven separate donations serviced by honest triggered tends can create seven tiny immature positions. [`_kickable()`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-automated-transmuter-strategy/blob/b6305a34c8ec99589982936b1b1cb1b5677a1b09/src/Strategy.sol#L565) then blocks auctions of substantial idle principal until maturity or intervention, while deposits may remain enabled. This requires favorable keeper ordering and costs the attacker donated tokens. Idle principal remains withdrawable; deployed automation was not tested.

### Recommendation

Preserve slots for material principal through position sizing, cadence controls or keeper ordering. Test repeated minimum donations interleaved with realistic auction and tend scheduling.

## [Low] Exact advertised withdrawals can fail through double rounding

### Details

[`_freeFunds()`](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-automated-transmuter-strategy/blob/b6305a34c8ec99589982936b1b1cb1b5677a1b09/src/Strategy.sol#L493) converts available liquidity down to MYT shares and redeems with another downward conversion. The exact virtual-share arithmetic can advertise 500 USDC but return 499.999999 USDC, causing default zero-loss `withdraw(maxWithdraw)` to revert. Smaller requests or suitable loss tolerance avoid the failure; the demonstrated discrepancy is dust.

### Recommendation

Derive the advertised limit from the actual redeemable share count and resulting asset output. Add a nonintegral exchange-rate boundary test.

## [Informational] Material mature-claim shortfalls remain unproven

### Details

The prepared Transmuter can return alAsset instead of MYT for a matured claim’s shortfall. The strategy’s limit models adjusted matured value as immediately redeemable MYT, while `_freeFunds()` ignores claim outputs. A material shortfall could therefore cause zero-loss reversion or burn shares against value retained for remaining holders. Ordinary tests acknowledge dust returns, but no valid protocol lifecycle demonstrated a material shortfall or established a general bound.

### Recommendation

Prove a quantified maturity-shortfall bound using stateful claims, repayments, burns, earmarking and backing losses. If material shortfalls are reachable, reconcile actual outputs without treating retained synthetic value as realized withdrawal loss.
