# InfiniFi Flex Looper review — PR #2 at `d58a6b0`

## [Medium] Funded redemption claims are accounted at the wrong exchange rate

### Details

InfiniFi burns queued iUSD when funding a request and records a fixed USDC entitlement. The strategy instead retains those receipts, reprices them at the current rate, and reconstructs retired receipts from collection proceeds. Rate changes between funding and collection therefore corrupt NAV and the reporting guard: 100 iUSD with 80 funded at $1 is worth $98 after a fall to $0.90, but the strategy values it at $90. Partial claims can prematurely clear pending accounting; price restoration can leave phantom claims. See [strategy accounting](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-flex-looper-strategy/blob/d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110/src/integrations/InfinifiStrategy.sol#L111) and [InfiniFi funding](https://github.com/InfiniFi-Labs/infinifi-protocol/blob/aeb468ebc18433f4b4bf62ed14c8bd271a0404c8/src/funding/RedemptionPool.sol#L56).

### Recommendation

Account separately for unfunded receipts and fixed funded USDC. Reconcile receipt retirement at funding, and test partial funding and collection across rate changes.

## [Medium] Lossy settlement permits withdrawals before losses are shared

### Details

`claimCooldown()` exposes recovered USDC without updating Tokenized Strategy’s cached NAV. If queued value falls from 100 to 90, a holder redeeming 50 of 100 shares before reporting receives 50, leaving 40 for the remaining holder instead of 45 each. InfiniFi implements receipt-price impairment, and neither the reporting guard nor withdrawal `maxLoss` prevents a full cash-funded payment at stale PPS. This requires competing holders or downstream withdrawals and non-atomic reconciliation. See [claim settlement](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-flex-looper-strategy/blob/d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110/src/integrations/InfinifiStrategy.sol#L184).

### Recommendation

Enforce settlement and accounting reconciliation before reopening affected share operations. Test two holders through a real impairment, including the parent Vault and rejected loss reports.

## [Medium] Redemption liquidity can block advertised withdrawals and reports

### Details

`availableWithdrawLimit()` checks InfiniFi queue length, then advertises equity using Flex and flash-loan capacity. An empty queue does not guarantee sufficient liquidity after redemption hooks: InfiniFi can pay partially and queue the remainder, while the strategy requires full immediate output and reverts. Admitted withdrawals and parent recalls can therefore fail. Over-limit reports also require deleveraging before valuation and can remain blocked by unavailable redemption; Flex cooldown creates an additional bounded reporting interruption. See [liquidity limits](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-flex-looper-strategy/blob/d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110/src/integrations/InfinifiStrategy.sol#L129) and [controller execution](https://github.com/InfiniFi-Labs/infinifi-protocol/blob/aeb468ebc18433f4b4bf62ed14c8bd271a0404c8/src/funding/RedeemController.sol#L105).

### Recommendation

Quote conservative executable liquidity, provide asynchronous recovery, and allow accurate reporting independently of unavailable maintenance. Test with unchanged redemption hooks and insufficient aggregate liquidity.

## [Medium] Full close requires external liquidity despite sufficient idle USDC

### Details

Both full-close entry points flash-borrow the entire debt and force collateral redemption without subtracting idle USDC. Consequently, an InfiniFi queue or insufficient flash liquidity can prevent closure even when the strategy already holds enough cash to repay all debt. Flex’s close operation itself only requires repayment and then releases collateral. The new active/zombie close selector fixes access to that operation but retains this avoidable dependency. See [close implementation](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-flex-looper-strategy/blob/d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110/src/Strategy.sol#L352).

### Recommendation

Use idle USDC first, flash-borrow only the shortfall, and convert only the remaining repayment deficit. Test queued redemption with idle cash exceeding debt.

## [Medium] Keepers can repeatedly consume principal through borrowing fees

### Details

Keepers can alternate leverage targets and call `tend()` directly. Reborrowing capitalizes fresh Flex upfront fees with unlimited fee tolerance; these losses bypass swap-loss counters and report health checks. A feasible equation model of 24 cycles between 2x and 3x, with a 10% weighted rate and seven-day upfront period, charges approximately 4.5% of initial equity in fees while respecting leverage bounds and repayment cooldowns. This requires a malicious or faulty keeper and sufficient liquidity. See [keeper targeting](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-flex-looper-strategy/blob/d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110/src/BaseLooper.sol#L174) and [borrowing](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-flex-looper-strategy/blob/d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110/src/Strategy.sol#L155).

### Recommendation

Enforce cumulative fee and turnover budgets, or require stronger authorization for material target changes. Validate repeated cycles against the composed contracts.

## [Medium] Maintenance can increase debt after shutdown

### Details

While the Trove remains active, keeper maintenance can supply collateral and borrow after irreversible shutdown because `_lever()` never checks shutdown. Borrowing resets Flex’s repayment cooldown and restores exposure during recovery. The acknowledged shutdown-and-close operating sequence protects only after closure succeeds; cooldown or redemption constraints can prevent immediate closure. See [maintenance branches](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-flex-looper-strategy/blob/d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110/src/BaseLooper.sol#L458).

### Recommendation

Prohibit risk-increasing maintenance after shutdown while retaining valuation, repayment and explicit collateral rescue. Test tending and reporting before close completes.

## [Medium] Management can redistribute principal through report discounts

### Details

Management can change `reportBuffer`, bypass health checks, admit controlled capital at an artificially depressed PPS, then restore valuation. With collateral 300, debt 200 and 100 shares, a 30% collateral discount reports 10 assets; a new 10-asset deposit receives half the shares and becomes worth 55 after restoration. This is an acknowledged management trust capability, requiring management compromise or hostility. See [mutable valuation](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-flex-looper-strategy/blob/d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110/src/BaseLooper.sol#L233).

### Recommendation

Remove discretionary discounts from canonical share accounting, or prevent intervening share operations while valuation changes are reconciled.

## [Medium] An unused exchange setter retains governance custody

### Details

The InfiniFi adapter uses Gateway conversions but inherits `setExchange()`. Immutable governance can approve an arbitrary spender for unlimited USDC and siUSD transfers, allowing loose principal to be removed outside conversion checks. This authority survives Tokenized Strategy management rotation. Governance is explicitly trusted, so exploitation requires its compromise or hostility. See [exchange approvals](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-flex-looper-strategy/blob/d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110/src/BaseLooper.sol#L275).

### Recommendation

Disable unused exchange configuration and approvals in this adapter. Make any necessary custody authority explicit and revocable.

## [Low] Ordinary deleveraging ignores Flex’s minimum debt

### Details

Flex caps ordinary repayment at `debt − MIN_DEBT`, but the strategy removes collateral sized from the requested repayment. Calls can revert or worsen posted leverage while remaining below the maximum; final withdrawals can be advertised but impossible. The new authorized full-close path provides an operational alternative, supporting Low severity under the stated seed/allocator assumptions. See [repayment callback](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-flex-looper-strategy/blob/d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110/src/BaseLooper.sol#L653).

### Recommendation

Size collateral removal from actual debt reduction, reflect the floor in withdrawal limits, and route below-floor exits through proper closure. Test parent recalls and final-holder exits.

## [Low] Underwater subtraction blocks idle-assisted recovery

### Details

`_lever()` subtracts posted debt before adding idle cash. Collateral worth 900, debt 1,000 and idle cash 200 therefore panic despite positive aggregate equity. Reporting reaches this branch when underwater leverage exceeds its maximum; pending assets can similarly fail to rescue the earlier base-estimator subtraction. Authorized repayment or liquidation offers alternatives, limiting severity. See [equity calculation](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-flex-looper-strategy/blob/d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110/src/BaseLooper.sol#L458).

### Recommendation

Aggregate backing before subtracting liabilities, handle insolvency explicitly, and keep valuation independent of mandatory rebalancing.

## [Low] Exhausted rewards still produce positive forward APR

### Details

The APR oracle treats InfiniFi’s entire escrow balance as rewards remaining to stream, although `vesting()` includes already vested rewards. After full vesting, `min(rate, escrow / elapsed)` remains positive despite zero future rewards; public distribution can then change the quote to zero without changing future entitlement. Material allocator impact remains unproved. See [APR formula](https://github.com/johnnyonline/yv3-flex-looper-strategy/blob/d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110/src/periphery/InfinifiAprOracle.sol#L30).

### Recommendation

Use remaining unvested rewards for a forward earning rate. Test exhaustion before and after distribution and validate the consuming allocator’s expected metric.

## [Informational] Deployment composition and stressed recovery remain unverified

### Details

These conclusions concern source at `d58a6b0bbd4a104c654af084863ea9794b8f3110`. Prepared evidence does not prove deployed bytecode, Gateway proxy implementation, actual oracle, role controls or concrete parent withdrawal policies. Compatible prepared Gateway V2/V3 implementations establish the integration mechanisms above; configuration also references unavailable V4 source. Stateful tests could not compile offline. At the proposed 110% MCR, 10x/10.5x leverage leaves approximately 1%/0.476% collateral-price margins before liquidation, excluding fees and interest, while cooldowns and queues can delay recovery.

### Recommendation

Verify the pinned build, runtime, proxy graph, oracle and parent default/custom queues at one fixed block. Execute real-hook liquidity, rate-changing claims, two-holder loss settlement, idle-funded closure, minimum-debt and full-cooldown liquidation tests before relying on the proposed configuration.
